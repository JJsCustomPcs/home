FAQ

Answers before your project starts.

Q: What kinds of builds are available?

A: Builds can be tailored for gaming, streaming, video editing, schoolwork, general use, or a mix of different needs.

Q: How do I request service or a quote?

A: Use the guided request form on the home page or email JJ with the project details. The form works for new builds, upgrades, repairs, and setup requests.

Q: Why choose JJ's Custom PCs?

A: Customers get straightforward part guidance, careful assembly, and service based on the actual system, budget, and goal.

Q: How did the service get started?

A: The service started from years of interest in gaming PCs, hardware research, and hands-on building, then grew into a customer-focused local PC service.

Q: How should a custom PC be maintained?

A: Keep vents clear, avoid blocking airflow, dust the system occasionally, and reach out if performance, temperature, or noise changes suddenly.
