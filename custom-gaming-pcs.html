Scope

What a custom build can include

The approved scope can cover planning through handoff, or assembly of a compatible parts list you already selected.

Component planning

CPU, graphics, motherboard, memory, storage, power, cooling, and case choices based on use, compatibility, value, and upgrade goals.

Assembly and cable routing

Careful installation, internal cable organization, fan placement, and a clean visible-side presentation.

Setup when approved

First boot, BIOS review, hardware detection, Windows and driver setup when included in the agreed work and a required license is available.

Basic checks

Power-on, storage detection, fan and RGB behavior, driver checks, and basic stability checks before handoff.

Planning priorities

What I plan around

Games and resolution

The games, target display resolution, and other applications help determine where the budget matters most.

Cooling and noise

Case airflow, cooler clearance, fan placement, and practical noise preferences are part of component planning.

Appearance

Case size, color, lighting, and visible cable layout can be coordinated without losing sight of compatibility.

Upgrade path

Power capacity, case clearance, platform choices, and available slots can leave room for later changes.

Process

From request to handoff

Request

Share games, applications, budget, preferred appearance, and any parts you own.

Assessment

I review goals, compatibility, cooling, value, and the service scope.

Approval

Parts and expected pricing are confirmed before work moves forward.

Build

I assemble the approved system and complete the agreed setup.

Testing

Hardware detection, drivers, fans, storage, and basic stability are checked.

Handoff

The finished system is handed off with relevant setup and warranty notes.
