Diagnostic scope

Problems I can investigate

Diagnosis identifies likely causes and practical next steps. A successful repair cannot be guaranteed before the system is assessed.

Boot and power issues

A desktop that will not power on, will not reach Windows, loops during startup, or stops during POST.

Instability

Crashes, freezes, unexpected restarts, or other symptoms that may involve hardware, drivers, cooling, or configuration.

Cooling symptoms

Unexpected fan behavior, thermal throttling, shutdowns under load, or temperatures that changed after hardware work.

Storage and performance

Drives that are not detected, slow startup, performance changes, or hardware-related storage concerns.

How the work is approached

Start with the symptom, not a promised fix

The existing JJ process starts with the request, reviews the system and service scope, confirms expected pricing before work moves forward, and performs the approved repair or component replacement. Hardware detection and basic stability are checked when relevant before handoff.
