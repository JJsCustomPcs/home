PC upgrade FAQ

Can I bring the upgrade part?

Yes. Customer-owned parts are supported, but compatibility depends on the existing system and the specific component.

Can every desktop accept a newer graphics card?

No. Power capacity, connectors, case clearance, platform support, and the rest of the system must be reviewed first.

What affects upgrade pricing?

The component, access and installation complexity, required troubleshooting, cable work, and the approved setup or testing scope.
