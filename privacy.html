Privacy Policy

Customer privacy is important to JJ's Custom PCs.

Information I Collect

I may collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, phone number, and details related to your PC quote requests.

How I Use Your Information

I use submitted information to understand the requested work, recommend suitable parts, respond to questions, and contact customers about their projects.

Cookies and Tracking

The site does not currently load an analytics service or use cookies for tracking. Its event wrapper sends nothing unless a real analytics tag is configured in the future.

Unfinished Quote Drafts

If JavaScript and browser storage are available, unfinished quote-form data may be stored locally in your browser for up to 24 hours so you can restore the draft. This draft stays on your device and is not submitted to JJ's Custom PCs or FormSubmit unless you choose to submit the form. You can remove it with the form's reset control or by clearing this site's browser data.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

Third-Party Services

Third-party services, such as FormSubmit, may be used to process quote requests. These services have their own privacy policies.
